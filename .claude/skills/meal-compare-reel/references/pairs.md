# The 310 comparisons

Every pair passes two tests: a person would genuinely choose between them, and the two plates look clearly different on screen. Top is placed first, bottom second. Both calorie figures go on screen.

**The gap runs both ways.** On the veg-vs-non-veg and protein boards many pairs have the vegetarian option coming out higher — paneer, malai, halloumi and cheese are heavier than the meat people compare them to. Those reversals are the strongest hooks.
