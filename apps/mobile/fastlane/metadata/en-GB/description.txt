LogMyPlate is an AI calorie counter for the food you actually eat. Take a photo of your plate and get calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds, whether it's a burger and fries, a chicken salad, a bowl of pasta, a sushi platter or a home-cooked curry. No searching endless food databases, no weighing every ingredient.

THREE WAYS TO LOG

Photo food scanner
Snap a photo or pick one from your gallery. The AI identifies each item on the plate, including mixed bowls and home-cooked dishes, and estimates calories and macros. Add a short note like "extra cheese" or "half portion" to sharpen the estimate.

Barcode scanner
Scan a packaged food for its nutrition facts and macros.

Type it in
No photo? Describe the meal the way you'd say it, like "2 eggs, toast and orange juice", and the AI breaks it down item by item.

FOOD FROM EVERYWHERE
British classics, Italian, Mexican, Japanese, Chinese, Thai, Mediterranean, Middle Eastern and Indian. From a full English to a meal deal to a Friday-night curry, point your camera and log it.

Grew up on dal, roti and biryani? LogMyPlate handles Indian home cooking as well as it handles a Sunday roast, so you can track the food you love wherever you live, not just the food that's easy to log.

MORE TO HELP YOU REACH YOUR GOAL

AI nutritionist chat
Ask questions like "Is this meal high enough in protein?" or "Suggest a 400-calorie snack" and get answers based on your goals.

Calorie and macro targets
Set daily calorie and macro targets from your activity level and goal, or choose your own split. Built for weight loss, a calorie deficit, muscle gain, intermittent fasting or maintenance.

You stay in control
AI estimates are a starting point. Adjust portions, edit items and change values before you save a meal.

Streaks and weekly journal
See your logging streak, weekly calorie averages and every meal in your journal. Keep your streak going to earn bonus scans.
