# Screenshot brief: first three screenshots

For Satyam. The first three screenshots are what most people see in search
results, so they carry the global positioning. Use the same three on iOS
(6.9" and 6.5") and Play (phone), with the caption above the device frame,
large and short (aim for 5 words or fewer on the headline).

## 1. Snap any meal, get calories

- **Caption:** "Snap any meal. Get calories."
- **Sub-caption:** "AI counts calories and macros in seconds"
- **Screen:** the review screen after a photo scan of a **burger and fries**
  (or a cheeseburger with a side salad), showing each item with calories and
  the protein / carbs / fat totals. Real food photo, good light, no Indian dish
  on this one.
- **Why:** the hero shot tells a US/UK/AU/CA visitor this app is for their food.

## 2. Food from everywhere

- **Caption:** "Pasta, sushi, curry: it's all here"
- **Sub-caption:** "From takeaway to home cooking"
- **Screen:** the weekly journal or Today screen with a day of mixed meals, for
  example a breakfast smoothie bowl, a **sushi** lunch, a **pasta** dinner and a
  **dal with roti** snack, each with its calories.
- **Why:** shows range across cuisines and quietly includes Indian home cooking
  for diaspora users, without making the listing read as India-only.

## 3. Ask your AI nutritionist

- **Caption:** "Ask your AI nutritionist"
- **Sub-caption:** "Answers based on your goals"
- **Screen:** the nutritionist chat with a short exchange such as "Is my lunch
  high enough in protein?" and a concise answer that references the logged
  meal. Keep the answer to 3 or 4 lines so it reads at thumbnail size.
- **Why:** the chat is the clearest difference from photo-only trackers.

## Notes

- Use a non-Indian name and avatar on screen (or none) and default units the
  app shows; don't show prices or free-scan counts (they change server-side).
- Light theme for 1 and 2, dark theme for 3, so the set doesn't look uniform.
- Screenshots 4 to 6 can cover barcode scan, macro targets and streaks.
- When ready, these can go in `fastlane/screenshots/<locale>/` for `deliver` and
  `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/` for `supply`.
